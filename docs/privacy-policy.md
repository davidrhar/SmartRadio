# Skipadoodle Privacy Policy (DRAFT, needs a public URL before Play submission)

*Last updated: 2026-10-05*

Skipadoodle is an Android internet-radio app published by Bonufied Pte Ltd.

**What the app does with your data**
- **No account, no ads, no analytics.** We run no server that receives your data and the app contains no
  tracking or crash-reporting SDK.
- **Your station list and order** are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall.
- **Audio.** To skip ads and talk, the app analyses the radio audio it is playing, on your device. The audio
  is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- **Location (optional).** Only if you tap "Near you" and grant approximate location, the app reads your
  phone's last known coarse location once to work out your country, so it can list popular local stations.
  The location is not stored or sent to us. Android may use an online geocoding service (provided by your
  device vendor, typically Google) to convert the coordinates to a country; that is governed by their policy.
  Only the resulting country code is sent to the station directory.
- **Station directory.** Searching sends your search text (and the optional country code) to the community
  Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). See their terms.
- **Streams and logos.** Audio and station logos load directly from each broadcaster's own servers, which can
  see your IP address and the fact that you are listening, as with any radio player. Some streams use
  unencrypted http because the broadcaster offers nothing else.

**Children.** Not directed at children under 13.
